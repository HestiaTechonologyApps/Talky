Privacy Policy for Talky

Effective Date: March 05, 2026

Hestia Technologies ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Talky mobile application (the "Service"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service and the choices you have associated with that data.

By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect several different types of information to provide and improve our Service to you.

Account Information

To use Talky, you must create an account. During registration, we collect the following information:

  • Mobile Number: Used strictly for account creation, authentication via OTP (One-Time Password), and account recovery.
  • Profile Details: Preferred display name, gender, profile picture, interests, and language preference. This information is displayed on your profile to other users within the app.

Device Permissions: Camera & Microphone

Talky is a real-time communication app. To function, we require explicit permission to access your device's Camera and Microphone.

  • Audio and Video Data: All video and audio calls are processed in real-time. We do not record, intercept, or store your audio or video streams on our servers. This data is strictly ephemeral.

Call Logs and Metadata

While we do not record the content of your calls, we do collect and store call logs on our servers for service functionality and history. This includes the caller ID, receiver ID, call duration, timestamps, and call status (e.g., missed, answered).

Contacts and Address Book

We do not request access to, collect, or store your device's contact list or address book. All connections are made directly through the Talky app's dashboard.

2. Third-Party Services

We employ third-party companies and SDKs to facilitate our Service, provide the Service on our behalf, and process payments. These third parties have access to your Personal Data only to perform these tasks and are obligated not to disclose or use it for any other purpose.

  • Agora: Used to facilitate and process live, real-time video and audio calls. Agora Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase (Google): Used for OTP SMS authentication and Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to deliver push notifications and incoming call alerts. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Razorpay: Used for processing in-app payments. We do not collect or store your sensitive payment details (like full credit card numbers). This data is provided directly to Razorpay, whose use of your personal information is governed by their privacy policy. Razorpay Privacy Policy.

3. User-Generated Content (UGC) and Safety

Talky relies on user-generated profiles and live interactions. To ensure a safe environment:

  • Age Restriction: Talky is strictly for users who are 18 years of age or older. Users must verify their age during account creation. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under the age of 18.
  • Block and Report: Users have the ability to block other users and report inappropriate behavior or profiles directly within the app. We actively monitor reports and reserve the right to ban users who violate our community guidelines.

4. Data Retention and Account Deletion

You have full control over your personal data and can request its deletion at any time.

  • How to Delete: You can delete your account directly inside the Talky app by navigating to your Profile Section > Delete Account. Alternatively, you can request account deletion by emailing us at the support address below.
  • Data Deletion Process: Upon initiating account deletion, your account is immediately deactivated and placed in a "soft delete" state for 30 days. After 30 days, your account, profile details, and associated call logs are permanently and irreversibly deleted from our servers.

5. Security of Data

The security of your data is important to us, but remember that no method of transmission over the Internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your Personal Data, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top.
